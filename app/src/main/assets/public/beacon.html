How to register your beacon?

  1. Hold down the red tactile button on your ESP32-S3 for 3 seconds until the blue LED blinks.
  2. Keep your smartphone within 2 meters of the device with Bluetooth enabled.
  3. Tap Scan for Nearby Devices above, select your device, and hit Register.
